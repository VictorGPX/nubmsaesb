About

Why the e-Suggestion Box?

Every student deserves the opportunity to be heard.

The NUBMSA E-Suggestion Box provides a platform to share ideas, raise concerns, report issues, and suggest areas where our department can do better. Whether you have a suggestion, need support, or wish to submit a complaint anonymously, your voice matters.

The goal is to encourage open communication, keep students informed, and ensure that their concerns and ideas contribute to decisions that affect them. Through REBUILD, RESTRUCTURE, and REIGNITE, we are creating room for every student to participate in shaping a better NUBMSA.

Your voice is part of our renewal.

F.A.Q

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the E-Suggestion Box truly anonymous?

Yes. You can submit suggestions, concerns, and complaints without providing your name or personal details. Anonymous submissions are intended to protect your identity.

Who will see my submissions?

Submissions will be reviewed by designated members of the NUBMSA administration responsible for receiving and addressing student feedback. Information will not be shared unnecessarily.

What happens after I submit a complaint?

Your submission will be reviewed, assessed, and directed to the appropriate person or office where necessary. Issues requiring further attention will be considered for appropriate action.

Will my complaint actually be addressed?

Every submission deserves attention. While not every suggestion or complaint can be implemented immediately, submissions will be reviewed and considered based on their relevance, urgency, and feasibility.

Can I report sensitive issues or concerns?

Yes. The platform provides an avenue to raise sensitive concerns that you may not feel comfortable discussing publicly. You can submit them anonymously.

Can I face any consequences for expressing my concerns?

Students should be able to express genuine concerns without fear of intimidation or victimisation. The platform is intended to encourage constructive feedback and responsible communication.

Can I suggest changes or new ideas without making a complaint?

Absolutely. You can share ideas, recommend improvements, suggest new initiatives, or identify areas where you believe the department can do better.

What kind of issues can I report or discuss?

You can raise concerns relating to academics, student welfare, departmental administration, communication, student activities, class representation, and other matters affecting the student experience.

Will I receive feedback after submitting a suggestion or complaint?

Where contact details are voluntarily provided, feedback may be communicated where appropriate. Anonymous submissions without contact details may not receive individual responses, but relevant issues will still be reviewed.

What if my concern is urgent?

The E-Suggestion Box provides a channel for receiving and reviewing student feedback. For emergencies or matters requiring immediate intervention, please contact the departmental Director of Welfare directly.
